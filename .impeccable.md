## Design Context

### Occasion onboarding reference, October 28
Owner rejected excessive planning and explicitly supplied an occasion-pill screen: strong centered question, short helper, wrapping soft pills, Explore and Skip. This is implemented in onboarding and /create with existing mint colors/fonts/stars and line icons instead of emojis. Six real registry types, including Christmas, Shopping and Other. Do not restore the old three-square-tile selector or replace this with generic task cards. Profile setup remains short and optional; no invented required photo/username/country.

### Latest owner reference, October 28, 2026
The owner supplied presently-gift-registry.html specifically for its progress card, Fraunces headings with Figtree body, wishlist page, and simple light/dark toggle. These are implemented. Preserve pale mint canvas #F0FDFA, mint actions #5EEAD4, Gravity Stars, and no decorative arrows. Do not import the prototype palette, mock businesses, product artwork or client-side reserve/mark-purchased behavior. Real confirmed quantities drive progress; temporary payment holds remain separate. A single icon switch replaces the appearance menu; system preference initializes new visitors until explicitly changed.

### Users
Families in Haiti and the diaspora sharing gift registries for weddings, babies, and birthdays. Mobile and constrained connections matter. Haitian Creole, French, and English have equal standing.

### Brand Personality
Welcoming, assured, celebratory. The user explicitly selected Contemporary Caribbean with ocean blue, sand, and bright coral. They rejected the forest-green boutique direction and arbitrary period in the wordmark.

### Aesthetic Direction
A contemporary Caribbean retail identity: strong ocean-blue typography, sunlit sand surfaces, coral calls to action and gift ribbon. Substantial sans-serif headings, readable body copy, and a tactile openable gift. Clear gifting purpose, confident composition, restrained line illustrations. No beige italic editorial template, decorative coordinates, arbitrary dots, stock beach imagery, AI imagery, or em dashes.

### Design Principles
1. Say gift registry immediately and make the next action obvious.
2. Build identity through composition, typography, and the dimensional gift, not a hue replacement.
3. Keep primary controls readable and usable on mobile, keyboard, touch, and reduced motion.
4. Show honest routes and available checkout behavior. Do not imply delivery, international money transfers, or automatic vendor payouts.
5. Preserve account, payment, and security behavior while improving presentation.

### Approved Direction, October 27, 2026
The owner explicitly selected A / Lakou with B / Atelye product detail. Family registry and local commerce lead; real maker attribution, useful product facts and seller/price/availability/fulfillment clarity follow. Soft controls. One researched original woven-basket 3D composition, subject to measured bundle and device tests, with accessible static equivalents. The old CSS gift box and circular carousel demo are not the approved direction. Do not present planning SVGs as finished 3D or real inventory.

### Latest owner feedback: direct interaction and page flow
The rotate button was explicitly rejected. Basket interaction belongs on the object: drag, touch, keyboard and inertia, with normal vertical scroll. Add meaningful scroll motion to scene and content. Bring arrows close to text (7-8px). The former pale coral/cream treatment was called generic and bland; current deeper marine/vermilion/cool-neutral refinement is implemented for review, not final approval.

### Researched composition implemented
Owner requested implementation. Homepage now uses a substantial ocean scene area, bright coral with dark ink primary labels, relaxed tracking, integrated light finder, single-merchant adaptation and differentiated sand-backed catalog. This is implemented for review, not owner sign-off. Keep transaction colors semantically separate. See docs/COLOR-UX-RESEARCH.md and latest HANDOFF entry.

### Approved mint accent, October 28, 2026
Owner explicitly chose Spectrum HSL 170.6 76.9% 64.3% as the brand color. This supersedes the former blue/coral accent choice. Use the exact mint for primary filled actions and logo detail, dark evergreen ink for labels, deeper same-hue links on light surfaces, mint links on dark surfaces, and restrained related neutral surfaces. Preserve semantic error/warning colors. Do not restore coral primary actions without owner direction.

### Pale mint canvas and Gravity Stars, October 28
Owner clarified the app background must be HSL 166.2 76.5% 96.7% (#F0FDFA), while keeping the approved mint button color. Light-mode canvas now uses that exact value. Dark mode remains available. Gravity Stars is the requested background interaction, integrated from Animate UI source behind content. Keep card/input surfaces opaque and controls readable; preserve reduced motion, pause/resume and bounded rendering.

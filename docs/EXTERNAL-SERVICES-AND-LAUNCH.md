# Bayou external services and launch evidence

Reviewed November 3, 2026. This is an operating checklist, not a compliance certificate. The first-year infrastructure budget remains $50 total. Use free services first, keep automatic paid upgrades disabled, and reserve domain money. A free quota is a limited pilot allowance, not a promise of continuous operation.

## Security and business acceptance gates

Use [OWASP ASVS 5.0](https://asvs.owasp.org/) to track technical controls with tests and evidence. A login fix does not establish all application security.

1. Configuration and identity: reject missing/placeholder credentials; keep secrets on the server; least OAuth scopes; PKCE/state and CSRF validation; verified provider email; generic, localized user errors; no automatic linking of unrelated accounts by matching email. Secure HttpOnly session cookies over HTTPS, fixed redirect origins, account/device revocation, current database authorization and admin MFA must pass on the integrated branch.
2. Authorization: test anonymous, normal user, wrong owner, merchant, suspended merchant, admin and superadmin for every protected read and mutation. Client role/owner/price fields are untrusted. Re-check roles and ownership in services, including after demotion, ban, deletion, session revocation and concurrent changes. Do not rely on hiding buttons or proxy role claims alone.
3. Money and stock: merchant is seller/payee, amounts/currency are server-derived integers, reservations and fulfillment are atomic, provider events verified and idempotent, out-of-order events safe, expiration/retries/reconciliation/refunds tested. Never infer paid status from the browser return URL. No live checkout activation before provider and settlement evidence.
4. Privacy and inputs: strict runtime validation, safe output encoding, no untrusted HTML, restricted external URLs/uploads, CSP and security headers, CSRF protection, private/unlisted/password registry rules, address encryption, scoped guest tracking, no shared private caches or sensitive log content. Test XSS/IDOR/SSRF cases and rate-limit outages.
5. Operations: append-only audit of sensitive admin/merchant/order changes, observability with redaction, incident and credential-rotation procedure, restore drills, bounded queues/retries, dependency remediation, quota alerts, rollback and a checkout kill switch. Budget tests and hosted load evidence remain separate from unit test counts.
6. Policy and legal review: publish truthful Bayou identity/contact details, merchant seller/delivery/refund/dispute terms, privacy notice, data purposes/access/deletion/retention procedure and subprocessors. Determine applicable consumer, privacy, tax and cross-border requirements with qualified local counsel based on actual markets and business activities. Do not assert that implementing a consent checkbox makes Bayou compliant in Haiti, the US, Canada, the Dominican Republic, Brazil or Chile. Provider KYC/AML requirements and contracts need actual merchant approval; a patente number field is not verification.

[Google's OAuth policies](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/policies) explicitly require separate deployment-tier projects and secure production configuration. These are account/operational tasks, not changes an unattended source repair loop can truthfully mark complete.

## Branch-specific evidence

The local port 3000 Claude design checkout is a different Prisma 6 lineage from codex/offline-completion (Prisma 7). Its login was repaired and verified through Google to the French dashboard. Do not apply either lineage's migrations to the other's database or claim the release branch's MFA/device encryption tests cover this design checkout. Integrate intentionally and retest before shipping. Existing owner data was not migrated, reseeded or copied by this repair.

The local quality loop checks source/build/audit only. Disposable PostgreSQL, actual browser/provider journeys, hosted configuration and legal review remain explicit release gates. No public deployment or payment activation is authorized by a passing local report.
